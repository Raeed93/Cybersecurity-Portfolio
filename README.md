# Cybersecurity Portfolio

Hands-on security work by **Raed Hassan**: audits, risk assessments, incident investigation, Linux, and SQL.

## About me

I'm a software developer with about three years of professional experience, moving into cybersecurity. I hold CompTIA PenTest+ and CompTIA A+ (Core 1), and I'm completing the Google Cybersecurity Certificate. As a developer, I've seen how systems are built and where weaknesses get in, and I want to use that understanding to protect people and the data they trust organizations with.
